Privacy Policy

Last updated September 7, 2026.

Overview

WRTE is an iOS app for place discovery and trip planning. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account — if you sign in, your sign-in provider shares a user ID and basic account details with us.
  • Usage — standard request data (such as device type and approximate region) to operate and secure the service.
  • On-device data — catalogs, favorites, and trip notes may stay on your device and iCloud.

How we use information

We use this information to provide discovery and planning features, authenticate you, improve the service, and respond to support. We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising inside WRTE.

Payments

If subscriptions are offered, they are billed by Apple. Apple handles payment details; we receive subscription status only.

Retention and choices

We keep account data while your account is active. You can delete content in the product, sign out, or ask us to delete your account from Support.

Children

WRTE is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the “Last updated” date when we do. Questions go through Support.
